Terms of Service

Last Updated: September 28, 2026
Submitting an Order Request records your acceptance of these Terms. Project work begins only after the required downpayment is successfully processed and verified.

1. Agreement to Terms

By proceeding with the initial payment, you (“the Client”) enter into a binding agreement with Benzel Delmo, operating under JUAN PROJECT WORKSPACE (“the Service Provider”). This agreement governs your use of the online workspace and the fulfillment of requested services.

2. Project Initiation & Downpayment

  • Initial Deposit: A non-refundable downpayment of 50% (unless otherwise specified in your invoice) is required to initialize your workspace and secure your place in the service queue.
  • Work Commencement: No design, development, or production work will begin until the downpayment is successfully processed and verified in the system.
  • Final Balance: Any remaining balance becomes due upon project completion or delivery and should be settled immediately.

3. Balance Due, Grace Period & Overdue Fees

  • Due Date: The Project End / Completion Date is also the Payment Due Date. Any remaining balance is due on that date and should be settled immediately.
  • 1-Day Grace Period: The first day after the due date is a grace period. No overdue fee is added on Day 1.
  • Overdue Status: If a balance remains unpaid after the grace period, the account may be marked overdue beginning on Day 2. No monetary overdue fee is charged on Day 2.
  • Initial Overdue Fee: If the balance remains unpaid on Day 3 after the due date, a ₱500 overdue fee is added.
  • 7-Day Overdue Fee: On Day 7 after the due date, an additional ₱250 is added. Another ₱250 is added for every succeeding 7-day period while a balance remains unpaid.
  • Example: If payment is due on September 14, September 15 is the grace day, September 16 may be marked overdue with no fee, September 17 (Day 3) adds ₱500, September 21 (Day 7) brings the total overdue fee to ₱750, and September 28 (Day 14) brings the total overdue fee to ₱1,000.
  • No Daily Fee: JUAN PROJECT does not charge a separate per-day overdue fee under this policy.
  • Partial Payments: A partial payment reduces the outstanding balance but does not stop the overdue-fee schedule while a balance remains unpaid. Overdue fees stop once the outstanding balance is fully settled.
  • Fee Waiver: JUAN PROJECT may waive overdue fees for a specific project when approved by the Service Provider.
  • Release of Final Assets: Final source files or restricted deliverables may be withheld until all outstanding balances and applicable overdue fees are fully settled.

4. Deliverables & Revision Policy

  • Scope: The Service Provider is only obligated to complete the specific Deliverables outlined and approved in your active workspace dashboard.
  • Revisions: Each Deliverable includes an allotted number of minor revision rounds as specified in the service package. Major structural changes requested after an initial concept is approved may be subject to additional billing.
  • Approval Window: Clients are expected to review Deliverables within 3 business days. Prolonged delays may result in the Deliverable being marked as auto-approved to keep the project timeline on track.

5. Loyalty Program & Rewards

Clients may earn points through the workspace loyalty system. Loyalty points and referral codes hold no direct cash value and may only be applied as discounts or perks within JUAN PROJECT Online. Points cannot be applied retroactively to already paid invoices.

6. Intellectual Property & Usage Rights

  • Ownership Transfer: Full usage rights for final approved Deliverables transfer to the Client only after final payment is received.
  • Working Files: Raw source files, backend code, and 3D project files remain the property of the Service Provider unless a specific Source File Transfer add-on was purchased.
  • Portfolio Rights: JUAN PROJECT WORKSPACE reserves the right to display completed Deliverables and early concepts in professional portfolios, case studies, and social media promotions.

7. Cancellation & Termination

If the Client cancels after work has commenced, the initial downpayment is forfeited to cover administrative and labor costs. If a project is canceled midway, the Service Provider may invoice for additional Deliverables completed and approved up to the date of cancellation.
